## 2. Recommended Daily Difficulty Distribution

**Best Choice:** Adjusted Option A

### Primary Distribution
> **3 Easy | 5 Medium | 1 Hard**

**This is the best match because:**
* You are beyond a beginner-only easy distribution.
* Medium questions provide the highest interview transfer.
* One hard question gives exposure without consuming the whole day.
* Two hard questions daily would reduce revision quality and likely weaken your explanation practice.
* Four easy questions every day would be useful for the first few tree/graph foundation days, but inefficient as the default.

### Temporary Adjustment
* **New Foundation Days:** Use **4 Easy | 4 Medium | 1 Hard** on days introducing a major new foundation, particularly early Tree/BST and Graph days.
* **10-Question Days:** Use **3 Easy | 6 Medium | 1 Hard** on the single 10-question day.
* **Standard Plan Warning:** Do not use Option C as your standard plan. Two hard questions can be used only when one is a previously studied hard problem being reattempted, not when both are new.

## 3. Three-Session Daily Structure

### Morning: Highest-Quality Reasoning
* **Target:** 3 questions (1 Easy + 1 Medium + 1 Hard)
* **Suggested Order:**
  1. Easy warm-up from a maintenance topic
  2. Medium from the day’s primary weak topic
  3. Hard from the same pattern family or a familiar hard problem

> **Note:** The hard question belongs in the morning, not at night, because it requires your strongest attention.

---

### Evening: Main Volume Session
* **Target:** 4 questions (1 Easy + 3 Medium)
* **Core Random-Cycling Session Example:**
  * Tree medium
  * Sliding-window medium
  * Array medium
  * Heap easy

*Use four different topic labels where possible.*

---

### Night: Consolidation
* **Target:** 2 questions (1 Easy + 1 Medium)
* Choose topics that emphasize implementation accuracy rather than heavy discovery.

#### Separate Review Block
* One problem from yesterday
* One problem from three days ago
* One problem from seven days ago
* One failed or editorial-assisted problem

> *Note: These review problems do not count toward the 127 new questions unless you are using a platform tracker that counts genuine re-solves separately.*

## Attempt Caps

| Difficulty | Time Limit |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Easy** | 15 – 20 minutes |
| **Medium** | 30 – 35 minutes |
| **Hard** | 45 minutes |

### Protocol When Stuck
1. If stuck, spend **5 additional minutes** writing exactly what is blocking you before checking a hint.
2. After viewing a hint or editorial, **close it** and reproduce:
   * The invariant or recurrence
   * The algorithm
   * The code
   * The complexity

> **Golden Rule:** A read editorial is not a completed problem.
